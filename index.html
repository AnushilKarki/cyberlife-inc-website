A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before we begin.

Can I engage you for just one service?

Yes. Projects can focus on software, branding, interior design, or construction. We confirm the relevant scope and team in the proposal.

Do you work with clients outside Nepal?

We welcome inquiries from Nepal, Australia, the UK, the USA, and other markets. Delivery arrangements and local technical requirements are agreed for each project.

How do you price a project?

We start with your goals and requirements, then prepare a scope and quotation. Cost depends on deliverables, complexity, dependencies, and timeline.

What information should I prepare?

A short description of your idea, preferred service, approximate timeline, and budget range is a useful start. Existing designs, systems, or drawings help us understand the work.
